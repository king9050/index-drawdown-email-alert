# Email Layouts

The subject must always start with:

`标普纳指回撤提醒`

Recommended subject:

`标普纳指回撤提醒｜{index summary}新触发{highest threshold}%｜{market date}`

## Chinese

1. Opening summary of newly crossed thresholds.
2. One block per triggered index:
   - market date
   - all-time high close and date
   - latest close
   - current drawdown
   - thresholds crossed in this run
   - thresholds alerted earlier in the cycle
3. Closing-price and investment-risk disclaimer.

## English

Use the same facts and order as the Chinese layout. Keep the Chinese subject prefix unchanged.

## Bilingual

Write the complete Chinese section first. Add a divider, then provide the complete English equivalent. Do not alternate sentence by sentence.
